# TacZ Roguelike Modpack

Welcome to the **TacZ Roguelike Modpack**! This modpack transforms your Minecraft world into an infinite-scaling roguelike shooter dungeon, fully integrated with the amazing **Timeless and Classics ZERO (TACZ)** framework.

![TacZ Roguelike Gameplay](https://via.placeholder.com/800x400?text=TacZ+Roguelike+Modpack)

## 🎯 Features

*   **Infinite Procedural Dungeons**: Every time you enter the dungeon, a new interconnected floor map is generated based on a coordinate system algorithm. Rooms contain different types of challenges, traps, and rewards.
*   **Intense Wave-Based Combat**: Engage varying levels of enemies in each room. The difficulty scales dynamically as you progress to higher floors.
*   **Stash & Extract System**: An advanced mechanic where dropped loot (weapons, ammo, and attachments) can be extracted safely to a unified stash. Die, and you might lose what you carried!
*   **Economy & Shop System**: Earn money by completing waves and selling duplicate gear. Use your hard-earned cash at Shop NPCs to reroll your loadout, buy ammo, or apply rare attachments.
*   **Performance Optimized**: Heavily configured to ensure smooth gameplay, featuring instant ghost-mob teleportation fixes and optimized server-side ticking for rapid room generation.

## 📥 Required & Recommended Add-ons

**Important Notice:** To strictly comply with copyright and redistribution rules, **this modpack does NOT include third-party gun pack jar files directly within the zip.**

While the modpack provides the base framework and logic, you **must/should download gun packs manually** for the best experience. We highly recommend adding the following packs to your `mods` folder after installation:

1.  **Elite X Quality Guns** (By Argos_Williams)
2.  **Daffa's Arsenal** (By daffas_)
3.  *Any other TACZ compatible addon.*

**How to Install Add-ons:**
1. Download the jar files from their official pages (e.g., CurseForge or Modrinth).
2. Place them into your profile's `mods` folder.
3. Launch the game and enjoy the expanded arsenal!
